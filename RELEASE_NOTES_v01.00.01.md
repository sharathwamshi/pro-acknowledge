# Key2View — Release Notes

## Version 01.00.01

### Apps & Websites tracking
- Applications and websites are now tracked and managed separately, each in its own tab under Settings.
- Websites are categorized by domain (e.g. youtube.com covers every page on the site) instead of individual links.

### Smarter categorization
- "Seen recently, not categorized" list surfaces apps and websites your team actually used but haven't been labeled yet — tag each as Productive, Unproductive, or Neutral in one click.
- Website entries are cleaned up automatically on add (removes https://, www, and page paths).
- Duplicate entries are blocked, invalid website domains are rejected, and a category must be chosen before saving.

### Confirmation & feedback
- Every add, re-label, and remove shows a confirmation message.
- Removing an app or website now asks for confirmation first.

### Reports
- New Reports screen with a custom date range.
- Team summary: people count, average productivity, productive hours, and present hours.
- Productivity by person, top applications, and top websites for the selected period.

### Excel export
- One-click export of the full report to a formatted Excel workbook with separate sheets for Summary, Attendance, Productivity, Applications, and Websites.

### Chart downloads
- Any chart can be downloaded as a PNG image for sharing in decks, emails, or documents.

### Version visibility
- The application version (v01.00.01) is now shown on the login screen and across all screens.

### Timezone fix
- Attendance and activity times now display in local time (IST) instead of UTC.
